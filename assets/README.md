# README banner artwork

## Approved asset

- File: [images/tahoe-banner.png](images/tahoe-banner.png).
- Canvas: 2172 × 724 pixels (3:1), PNG.
- Purpose: header for `Tahoe`.
- Creation: OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, directed and iteratively reviewed by the human maintainer.
- Approval: the maintainer explicitly approved the Sequoia and final Tahoe banners in the project conversation.
- Validation: visible text and composition reviewed; PNG fully decoded; dimensions and SHA-256 checked before publication.

The Sequoia composition, typography and icon arrangement are retained with a cool cyan/blue/lavender palette and restrained clear-glass highlights. The final revision balances transparency and reflections without the earlier bulky glass treatment.

The maintainer selected the icon-mosaic direction, requested less glass, then requested a closer match to Sequoia. A new Tahoe companion was generated from the approved Sequoia artwork, followed by the final clear-glass refinement below. The maintainer approved that final revision. Earlier alternatives are not shipped.

## AI provenance and prompt

This artwork was generated with AI under direct human instruction and approval. Approval concerns the visual design; it is not a legal clearance or hardware validation. Generation is nondeterministic, so rerunning a prompt does not reproduce identical bytes.

The immediate edit input was the flat Tahoe companion derived from the approved Sequoia banner. Its SHA-256 is `86f4455fc703e093a0f31718b78382d841719f3b146f710770b4c32b625c89b1`. The accepted Sequoia source SHA-256 is `13362a41dd5e8c01369f2240ad0c5e27f8b800ba37521a9be35563e8db3a836e`. These identify the actual edit lineage; the final prompt alone is not a complete deterministic reconstruction.

The final edit prompt follows:

```text
Edit this Tahoe banner with a carefully balanced increase in Liquid Glass. The user likes the layout, matching their Sequoia banner, but says this version looks frosted/frozen and wants a little MORE clear glass.

ONLY change the icon materials. Preserve the precise 3:1 canvas, identical composition, icon sizes and positions, pastel cyan/blue/lavender palette, sweeping background shapes and calm bright center. Preserve the three dark matte centered text lines exactly in font, size, spacing and position: "HP86F3-HACKINTOSH", "Tahoe", "macOS compatibility research".

Give the icons clear, smooth, thin optical-glass surfaces rather than milky/frosted silhouettes. Background colors and shapes should remain visibly sharp through their interiors. Add controlled subtle refraction at the edges, delicate bright highlights along only selected upper edges, very thin blue refractive rims along selected opposite edges, and faint soft grounding shadows. This is a subtle two-dimensional UI glass material with just a hint of thickness, not bulky extruded 3D glass sculpture. Most of the icon remains quietly transparent. Make the glass effect clearly visible on the window upper right, folder lower left, monitor border upper left, terminal lower right and mouse. Apply the same restrained clear finish to command symbol, gear, and keyboard. The effect should be intermediate: unmistakably smooth clear glass, but elegant and quiet enough to match a flat pastel Sequoia companion banner.

Important: remove the cloudy white frosted appearance. No icy textures, frost, frosted blur, condensation, thick beveled edges, heavy gloss, metallic shine, neon outlines, strong rainbow aberrations, or bubble-like swollen forms. Do not add new icons or cursor. Do not tilt or move anything. Keep the headline absolutely matte and strongly legible; do not turn the lettering into glass. Deliver ONE refined finished banner.
```
